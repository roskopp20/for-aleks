aim

            

              The aim is to draw a picture using physics, not a camera.
              There is a lamp on the ceiling. The computer fires pretend rays of light,
              lets them bounce off the walls and the two balls, and paints whatever colour
              actually arrives at your eye. That is what a path tracer is for:
              to answer “if this room were real, what would it look like?”

the nerd bit

            

              Light leaving a surface = light it emits + light that bounced in from every direction.
              That sentence is this formula:
            

            

              Lo(x,ωo) = Le(x,ωo) + ∫Ω
